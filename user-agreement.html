Eramora is a personal life-record application designed to help users create, organise, preserve and explore information about their lives.

Features may include, depending on the version of the App:

  • Moments and stories;
  • People;
  • Places;
  • Tags;

3. Privacy and Local Storage

Eramora is designed around a private, offline-first architecture. Your personal Eramora archive is primarily stored locally on your device rather than on servers operated by Eramora or Kemp Digital.

The initial version of Eramora does not require an Eramora account and does not provide Eramora-operated cloud storage or automatic cloud synchronisation of your personal archive.

More information about how information is handled is available in the Eramora Privacy Policy.
