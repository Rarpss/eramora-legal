Eramora Data & Deletion

Version 1.0
Effective: 14 September 2026

Important: Eramora does not require an account and does not store your personal life archive on Eramora-operated servers. Your archive is stored locally on your device unless you deliberately export or back it up elsewhere.

1. No Eramora Account to Delete

Eramora does not currently use user accounts for its personal archive.

There is therefore no Eramora account, profile or cloud archive that must be deleted from Kemp Digital's servers when you stop using the App.

Your personal Eramora archive is stored locally on your device.

2. What Data Is Stored Locally

Depending on how you use Eramora, local data may include:

  • Moments;
  • stories and notes;
  • dates and date ranges;
  • People;
  • Places;
  • Tags;
  • Chapters;
  • photos and supported attachments;
  • drafts;
  • Favourite and Milestone status;
  • relationships between archive records;
  • local app settings; and
  • other archive information you choose to create or import.

This data is not stored on servers operated by Eramora or Kemp Digital as part of normal use.

3. Delete Individual Records

Eramora allows users to delete supported archive records from within the App.

For example, where available, you may delete individual Moments or other records using the applicable delete controls in Eramora.

Eramora Version 1 uses permanent deletion rather than a cloud recycle bin or account-level recently deleted system.

Before deleting important information: consider creating a backup if you may want to restore the information later.

4. Delete Your Entire Local Eramora Archive

Because your archive is stored locally, deleting all Eramora data is performed on your own device rather than by sending a server-side account deletion request to Kemp Digital.

Depending on your device and operating system, you can remove your local Eramora archive by using the App's available data-management controls or your device's application-management functions.

This may include actions such as:

  • deleting records from within Eramora;
  • clearing Eramora's application data through your device settings, where supported; or
  • uninstalling Eramora where the operating system removes the App's private local data.

The exact steps vary by operating system and device version.

5. Backups Are Separate

Deleting Eramora from your device does not necessarily delete backups or exports that you previously created and saved elsewhere.

Backups and exports may exist in locations such as:

  • local device folders;
  • external storage;
  • a computer;
  • a network-attached storage device;
  • Google Drive;
  • OneDrive;
  • iCloud Drive; or
  • another location you selected.

These files remain under your control and must be deleted from those locations separately if you no longer want them.

If you saved a backup to a third-party cloud provider, you should use that provider's own deletion tools to remove it.

6. Exported Files Are Separate

Eramora may allow you to create exports such as structured archive files or human-readable documents.

Once an export is saved outside Eramora's private application storage, it is no longer automatically controlled by the App.

You are responsible for deleting exported files from any location where you saved or copied them.

7. Information Sent Directly to Kemp Digital

Although Kemp Digital does not normally receive your Eramora archive, you may voluntarily send information to us, for example through:

  • support emails;
  • feedback;
  • screenshots;
  • technical information; or
  • other files you choose to attach to a message.

If you want to request deletion of personal information that you have directly provided to Kemp Digital, contact:

eramorasupport@gmail.com

Please provide enough information for us to identify the relevant correspondence or information.

We may need to retain limited information where reasonably necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent misuse or maintain necessary business records.

8. Purchase Records

If Eramora offers paid features through Google Play, the Apple App Store or another authorised application marketplace, the marketplace provider may retain purchase and transaction records under its own terms and policies.

Kemp Digital cannot directly delete records that are independently held by Google, Apple or another marketplace provider.

You should contact or use the privacy controls of the applicable provider for requests relating to information held by that provider.

9. Map and Catalogue Downloads

Eramora may download optional map or geographic catalogue resources.

These resources are separate from your personal life archive and may be removable through Eramora's data-management controls or through your device's application-storage controls, depending on the version and platform.

10. Data Recovery

Because Kemp Digital does not keep a server-side copy of your personal archive, we generally cannot recover local Eramora data after it has been permanently deleted, lost or removed from your device.

Recovery may only be possible if you have retained a valid Eramora backup or another independent copy of the relevant information.

11. Privacy Policy

For more information about how Eramora handles information, please read the Eramora Privacy Policy.
