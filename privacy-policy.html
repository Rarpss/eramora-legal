Eramora Privacy Policy

Version 1.0
Effective: 14 September 2026

Privacy by design: Eramora is designed so that your personal life archive stays on your device. Eramora does not require an account and does not upload your Moments, stories, photos, People, Places, Chapters, Tags or other personal archive content to Eramora-operated servers.

1. About This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Eramora ("Eramora", "the App").

Eramora is developed and operated in Australia under the name Kemp Digital.

Eramora is designed as a private, offline-first application for creating, organising, preserving and exploring a personal life archive.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Eramora's privacy practices, you can contact: eramorasupport@gmail.com.

2. Your Personal Archive

Eramora stores your personal archive locally on your device. Personal archive content is not uploaded to or stored on servers operated by Eramora or Kemp Digital.

Depending on how you use the App, your local archive may contain information such as:

  • Moments and their titles;
  • stories and notes;
  • dates and date ranges;
  • People and information you record about them;
  • Places and custom locations;
  • Tags;
  • Chapters;
  • photos and related metadata;
  • Favourite and Milestone status;
  • relationships between archive records;
  • drafts;
  • application settings; and
  • other information you choose to add to your archive.

This information may be highly personal or sensitive depending on what you choose to record. Eramora is designed so that this content remains under your control on your device unless you deliberately export, back up or otherwise share it.

3. No Eramora Account or Cloud Archive

Eramora does not require you to create an Eramora account.

The initial version of Eramora does not provide Eramora-operated:

  • cloud journal storage;
  • cloud photo storage;
  • automatic cloud backup;
  • cross-device cloud synchronisation; or
  • online storage of your personal life archive.

Because your archive is not stored on Eramora-operated servers, Kemp Digital cannot normally access, view or recover your personal archive.

4. Local Storage and Security

Eramora uses local application storage to maintain your archive, including a local encrypted database and private application storage for supported photos and attachments.

Eramora is designed to provide technical protections for locally stored information. However, no software, device or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

The security of your archive may also depend on the security of your device, operating system, device passcode, backups and any locations to which you export your information.

5. Photos and Attachments

If you choose to add photos or supported attachments to Eramora, the App creates and stores the necessary copies within its private local application storage.

Eramora may process photos locally on your device, for example to create optimised versions or thumbnails. This processing does not require your personal archive photos to be uploaded to Eramora-operated servers.

You control which photos or attachments you choose to import into Eramora.

6. Places and Location Information

Eramora allows you to associate Places with Moments and other parts of your life archive.

Eramora is not designed as a passive location-tracking service. It does not build your life archive by continuously tracking where you travel.

Locations represented in your life archive are based on Places that you deliberately select, create or associate with your records.

Eramora may provide an offline geographic catalogue and offline map data to support place searching and the Life Map. These resources are separate from your personal archive.

7. Backup and Restore

Eramora allows you to create user-controlled backups of your archive.

You choose where a backup is saved using the storage and file-selection functionality available on your device.

For example, depending on your device and installed services, you may choose to save a backup to local storage, external storage, a computer, a network location or a third-party cloud storage provider.

If you choose to save an Eramora backup using a third-party service such as Google Drive, OneDrive or iCloud Drive, that service is storing the file at your direction. Eramora and Kemp Digital do not operate that third-party storage service.

Your use of a third-party storage provider is subject to that provider's own terms and privacy practices.

8. Exports

Eramora is designed to give you long-term ownership of your information. The App may allow you to export your archive or selected parts of it in formats such as structured data files and human-readable documents.

Exported files are created at your request. Once an export leaves Eramora's private application storage, you are responsible for deciding where it is stored, copied or shared.

Exported files may contain personal or sensitive information. You should consider this before sharing them or saving them to locations accessible by other people or services.

9. Analytics, Advertising and Crash Reporting

Eramora Version 1 does not use a remote analytics service to collect information about how you use your personal archive.

Eramora Version 1 also does not use a remote crash-reporting service that sends application crash reports to Kemp Digital.

Eramora does not use your personal archive content for advertising.

If analytics, diagnostic reporting, advertising or similar technologies are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate before or alongside that change.

10. Internet Access and Downloads

Normal creation, editing, browsing, searching and filtering of your Eramora archive is designed to work without an internet connection.

Internet access may nevertheless be used for functions outside the storage of your personal archive, including:

  • downloading and installing Eramora;
  • application updates;
  • optional downloadable map or catalogue resources;
  • accessing external support or legal pages;
  • app-store functionality; and
  • purchases or restoration of paid features where applicable.

11. Purchases and Paid Features

Eramora may offer optional paid features or upgrades.

Where purchases are offered through Google Play, the Apple App Store or another authorised application marketplace, payment processing and associated store transactions are handled by the applicable marketplace provider rather than directly by Kemp Digital.

Those providers may process information relating to your purchase in accordance with their own terms and privacy policies.

Eramora may receive or locally retain information necessary to determine whether you are entitled to use purchased features, such as purchase or entitlement status. Eramora does not need access to your complete payment card details to provide app-store purchases.

12. Information You Send to Us

Although Eramora does not normally receive your personal archive, you may choose to contact Kemp Digital, for example by email for support, feedback or another enquiry.

If you contact us, we may receive the information you voluntarily provide, such as:

  • your email address;
  • your name, if included;
  • the contents of your message;
  • screenshots or files you choose to attach; and
  • technical information you voluntarily provide for support.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal archive information unless it is necessary for your support request.

Information you provide directly for support or correspondence will be used for purposes such as responding to your enquiry, troubleshooting problems, maintaining necessary correspondence and protecting the legitimate operation of Eramora.

13. Third-Party Services

Some aspects of obtaining or using Eramora may involve services operated by third parties, such as your device operating system, application marketplace, payment provider or a storage provider you independently choose for exported files or backups.

These services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Kemp Digital does not control how independent third-party services handle information that you provide directly to them.

14. Data Sharing and Sale

Kemp Digital does not sell your Eramora personal archive or personal information.

Because Eramora's personal archive is stored locally rather than on Eramora-operated servers, Kemp Digital does not have a server-side copy of your archive to sell or disclose in the ordinary operation of the App.

Information voluntarily provided to Kemp Digital, such as support correspondence, may only be disclosed where reasonably necessary to operate or protect the service, comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, or with your consent.

15. Data Retention and Deletion

Local Eramora archive

Your local archive remains on your device until you delete information, clear the App's data, uninstall the App where the operating system removes its private data, restore or replace your archive, or otherwise manage the data through your device.

Because Kemp Digital does not store your personal archive on Eramora-operated servers, Kemp Digital generally cannot remotely delete or recover that local archive for you.

Backups and exports

Backups and exports saved outside Eramora's private application storage remain wherever you chose to save them until you delete them from those locations.

Information sent directly to Kemp Digital

Support correspondence and similar information sent directly to Kemp Digital may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the enquiry, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes, protect the service or comply with legal obligations.

You may contact eramorasupport@gmail.com regarding personal information that you have directly provided to Kemp Digital.

16. Children

Eramora is a general-purpose personal life-record application and is not specifically designed, directed or marketed toward children.

Eramora's core personal archive is stored locally on the user's device and is not submitted to Eramora-operated servers as part of normal use.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has directly provided personal information to Kemp Digital, such as through a support request, you may contact eramorasupport@gmail.com.

17. Your Choices and Control

Eramora is designed to give you substantial control over your information. Depending on the feature, you can:

  • choose what information to add to your archive;
  • edit or permanently delete archive records;
  • choose which photos to import;
  • choose which Places to associate with Moments;
  • create backups;
  • choose where backups and exports are saved;
  • export your information; and
  • remove the App and its local data using your device's available controls.

18. Australian Privacy and Consumer Rights

Eramora is developed and operated in Australia. Nothing in this Privacy Policy is intended to exclude any privacy, consumer or other legal rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Depending on your location and the circumstances, applicable law may provide additional rights relating to personal information.

Requests or questions concerning personal information held directly by Kemp Digital can be sent to eramorasupport@gmail.com.

19. International Users

Eramora may be made available to users outside Australia.

The core Eramora archive remains locally stored on the user's device regardless of the user's location. Third-party services independently selected or used by the user may process information in other countries according to their own practices.

Eramora may change over time, and this Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect new features, technologies, legal requirements or privacy practices.

When this Privacy Policy is updated, the version number and effective date at the top of this page will be updated.

Where appropriate, material privacy changes may also be communicated through the App, application marketplace or another reasonable method.
